Delete Account / Data

Momo Merge: Cute Fruit Drop · Imperial Solutions · 06 November 2026

1. Quick facts

  • Guest play needs no account at all. If you never signed in, everything is on your device and erasing the game (Android Settings → Apps → Momo Merge → Clear data, or clearing your browser storage) deletes all of it.
  • Sign-in is optional. If you signed in with Google, a small account exists on Supabase (a hosted database) holding your display name, scores and an account ID.

2. How to delete your account data

  1. Email contact@imperialsolutions.org from the Google address you signed in with.
  2. Say you want your Momo Merge data deleted (listing only your score history is also fine — that keeps your account). Subject line above is pre-filled, no special form is needed.
  3. We verify the request against the address used and process it within 30 days, confirming by email.

You can also disconnect the game on your own at any time — without emailing us — at Google Account → Security → Third-party access & connections (remove Momo Merge / Imperial Solutions).

3. What we delete, what we keep

Deletion type Deleted Kept
All data Display name, all scores (all-time / daily / weekly), account ID row Nothing on our side
Scores only Score rows and leaderboard entries Account identity until you ask again

Local progress stored on your own device is never on our servers; deleting the app's data removes it entirely and immediately.

4. AdMob / Google ad data

Ad data handled by Google AdMob is not stored in our systems, so we cannot delete it for you. Personalized-ads consent can be withdrawn from the app's privacy options entry point, and you can reset your advertising ID in Settings → Google → Ads.

5. Retention

Until you ask, account data is kept only while useful for the cloud-save and leaderboard features. There is no additional retention period after a completed deletion request.
