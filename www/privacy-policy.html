1. Summary

Momo Merge: Cute Fruit Drop ("the App") is built to respect your privacy. The App does not require an account, does not ask for your name, email, or phone number, and does not collect personal information. You can play the entire game without telling us anything about yourself.

2. Information stored on your device

To remember your progress between sessions, the App stores the following locally on your own device (using your browser's or the app's local storage):

  • Your high score and best combo.
  • In-game currency (diamonds), owned power-ups, and achievements.
  • An optional display name you may choose. This is stored only on your device and is never transmitted to us.

This data never leaves your device. Clearing the app's data (or your browser data) deletes it permanently. We have no servers that receive it.

3. Advertising

The App is free and supported by advertising. Ads are shown by third-party advertising providers, which may independently collect limited technical information to serve and measure ads, such as a device advertising ID, approximate location (country/city level), device model, and ad-interaction data.

  • On Android (Google Play): ads are served by Google AdMob. See Google's Privacy Policy and how Google uses information. You can opt out of personalized ads in your device settings (Google → Ads → Opt out of Ads Personalization).
  • On web portals (e.g. CrazyGames, GameDistribution, itch.io): ads are served by that portal's own advertising system, subject to the portal's privacy policy.

We do not sell your personal information, because we do not collect any.
